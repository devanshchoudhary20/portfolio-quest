Portfolio Quest battle art

The nine non-champion trainers (core, design, monorepo, cross, backend, dsa, machine-coding,
system-design, behavioral) are original full-body drawings. Their hair, colours and outfit cues
are taken from the matching Ninja Adventure Asset Pack characters by Pixel-Boy (CC0 1.0,
https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack). Credit is not required by CC0 and is
given anyway.

Creatures, battle backgrounds and the champion placement (devansh.png, from the project's own
avatar sprite) are original work, drawn by script (.anbu/design/battle/). They contain no
marks of any game publisher, or of React, Node.js or Anthropic.

Everything in this folder is released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
